HotelFlow
1. Project Overview

Project name: HotelFlow

The problem: Independent and small hotels in Lebanon (roughly 10–80 rooms) manage bookings and room readiness through phone calls, WhatsApp, paper logs, or Excel, with verbal handoffs between reception and housekeeping. This causes booking mistakes, outdated room status, unprepared rooms, lost messages, and wasted time coordinating between shifts.

Target users: Managers, receptionists, and housekeeping staff at independent and small hotels that rely on manual or informal workflows and don't have a dedicated hotel operations system. The hotel manager/owner is the paying customer; receptionists and housekeeping staff are the daily users.

Proposed solution: A simple web-based hotel operations system that helps one small hotel manage reservations, room status, check-in/check-out, and housekeeping handoffs in one shared place.

2. Approved MVP

Core features:

Reservation management — create, view, edit, and cancel reservations

Room status management — track room state (available, reserved, occupied, needs cleaning, ready)

Check-in and check-out — update guest and room workflow status

Housekeeping handoff — rooms automatically flagged for cleaning after check-out, cleared once housekeeping marks them ready

Out of scope (postponed):

Guest self-booking / public booking platform

Online payments

Inventory management

Maintenance tracking

POS / accounting

Advanced analytics, AI features

OTA integrations

Multi-property / multi-tenant support

Advanced offline behavior

Current project status: Planned — Assignment 1 (Product Discovery & Market Validation) revised per mentor feedback; scope narrowed to a single-pilot-hotel MVP. Implementation has not started. Pilot hotel confirmation and additional interviews/surveys in progress.

6. Contribution Workflow
Task branches: Create a branch per task/feature (e.g. feature/reservation-crud, fix/room-status-bug). Do not commit directly to main.

Meaningful commits: Write clear, descriptive commit messages that explain what changed and why.

Pull requests: Open a PR from your task branch into main (or the agreed integration branch) once the task is complete and tested locally.

Peer review: At least one other team member reviews and approves a PR before it is merged.
